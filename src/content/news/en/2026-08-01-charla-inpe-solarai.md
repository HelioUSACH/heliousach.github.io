Dr. Victor Pinto gave an invited talk at the **GSST/CBERS-5 SolarAI Symposium on Machine Learning in Heliophysics and Space Weather**, organized by Brazil's National Institute for Space Research (INPE).

The group collaborates with INPE on the CNPq project on geomagnetically induced currents in Brazil and particle precipitation in the South Atlantic Anomaly.
