---
title: Chile's first space weather platform
date: 2025-12-02
summary: The FONDEF IDEA project, led by Universidad Adventista de Chile with USACH as partner institution, aims to anticipate solar events that affect critical technologies.
---

The **FONDEF IDEA I+D** project was presented. It develops a pilot platform for real-time space weather monitoring and early warnings in Chile, led by **Universidad Adventista de Chile** (director: Manuel Bravo) with **USACH** as partner institution, where Dr. Victor Pinto is principal investigator.

The platform will combine satellite and ground magnetometer data with deep-learning forecasting models. The launch was covered by Diario Usach, Cooperativa Ciencia, Crónica Digital, and other outlets.
