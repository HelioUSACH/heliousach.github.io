## 1. Tone and Voice

**Problem:** The current site reads like a startup/commercial landing page. "Donde termina el Sol, empieza el clima espacial" is a slogan, not research writing.

**Fix:** Academic, direct, descriptive. The hero should state what the group is and what it studies, period. Remove taglines. Use language a visiting researcher would expect.

**Before:**
> "Donde termina el Sol, empieza el clima espacial."
> "De la ciencia básica a la infraestructura crítica"

**After:**
> "Heliofísica y Clima Espacial — Universidad de Santiago de Chile"
> Hero subtitle: "Grupo de investigación en heliofísica y clima espacial del Departamento de Física de la Universidad de Santiago de Chile. Estudiamos la magnetosfera terrestre, los cinturones de radiación, la turbulencia en plasmas espaciales y desarrollamos herramientas de predicción de clima espacial."

## 3. Actual Research Lines

The current three lines are inaccurate and incomplete. The real research lines, based on publications, grants, and the group's actual work:

### 3.1. Radiation Belt Dynamics

**Who:** V. Pinto (PI), M. Stepanova, A.M. Inostroza, B. Zenteno-Quinteros

**Topics:**
- Energization, transport, and decay of relativistic electrons in the Van Allen belts
- Enhancement and dropout events; radial response during geomagnetic storms
- Formation, evolution, and decay of ultrarelativistic remnant belts
- Adiabatic and non-adiabatic processes in the outer radiation belt
- Auroral phenomena and their influence on radiation belt dynamics

**Data:** Van Allen Probes (MagEIS, REPT, EMFISIS, EFW), GOES, THEMIS

**Key publications:** Pinto et al. 2020 (JGR), Pinto et al. 2025 (Phys. Scr.), Inostroza et al. 2026 (JGR), Stepanova et al. 2024 (Rev. Mod. Plasma Phys.)

### 3.2. Magnetospheric Turbulence and ULF Waves

**Who:** M. Stepanova (PI), V. Pinto (Co-I), C.M. Espinoza

**Topics:**
- Electromagnetic ULF wave power and its effect on trapped charged particles
- Turbulence in the interconnected dynamic magnetosphere
- Total pressure balance in the magnetosphere during storms and substorms
- Eddy diffusion coefficients in the plasma sheet (THEMIS observations)
- Wave-particle interactions

**Data:** Magnetometers (SAMBA, MACCS, THEMIS GBO, CARISMA), THEMIS, SuperDARN

**Key publications:** Stepanova & Pinto 2026 (Phys. Scr.), Pinto et al. 2011 (JASTP), Lara et al. 2025 (Universe)

### 3.3. Space Weather Prediction (ML)

**Who:** V. Pinto (PI), M. Stepanova (Co-Director), A. Keesee (collaborator, UNH)

**Topics:**
- Ground magnetic field perturbation forecasting
- Geomagnetically Induced Currents (GIC) prediction
- Probabilistic forecasting of geomagnetic storm effects
- ML model explainability for space weather (SHAP, LIME)
- Deep learning (LSTM, CNN, RNN) for near real-time prediction
- Plasmapause location with ML

**Data:** OMNI (real-time), SAMBA magnetometer chain, THEMIS, GOES

**Key publications:** Pinto et al. 2022 (Front. Astron. Space Sci.), Coughlan et al. 2025 (Space Weather), Coughlan et al. 2023 (Space Weather)

### 3.4. Complex Systems and Solar Wind — Magnetosphere Coupling

**Who:** M. Stepanova, V. Pinto

**Topics:**
- Solar wind dynamic pressure and its effect on magnetospheric turbulence
- Solar wind — magnetosphere — ionosphere coupling
- Solar wind adiabatic invariants and expanding plasma systems
- Foreshock transients and their impact on magnetospheric perturbations (NASA LWS)

**Data:** OMNI, THEMIS, Van Allen Probes

**Key publications:** Stepanova et al. 2026 (Phys. Scr.), Moya et al. 2024 (ongoing), Antonova et al. 2023 (Springer)

## 5. Actual Labs

**The group operates three labs. The current site lists only HPC and the space weather platform as if they were the labs.**

### 5.1. Laboratorio de Instrumentación Espacial

- Ground-based magnetometer network (SAMBA chain, Chile)
- Magnetometer deployments and calibration
- Antarctic field campaigns (Chilean Antarctic Expedition 47, 2011)
- Real-time geomagnetic data acquisition
- Comparative magnetometer performance analysis (Universidad Adventista de Chile deployment, Aug 2025)
- Collaboration with MACCS, THEMIS GBO, CARISMA networks

### 5.2. Laboratorio de Clima Espacial

- Real-time space weather monitoring
- ML-based forecasting of GIC, geomagnetic perturbations, relativistic electron fluxes
- The national space weather platform (FONDEF IDEA I+D)
- Collaboration with Universidad Adventista de Chile
- Near real-time OMNI data integration

### 5.3. Plataforma HPC de Cómputo de Alto Rendimiento

- FONDEQUIP 2023 project (PI: V. Pinto)
- HPC clusters for numerical simulations, data science, plasma modeling
- Supports all HelioUSACH research groups and external USACH researchers
- Separate infrastructure from the two research labs
